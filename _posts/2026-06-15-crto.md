## the cert

CRTO is Zero-Point Security's red team course and exam, built around Cobalt Strike as the C2. It covers the full chain: initial access, host and Active Directory enumeration, privilege escalation, lateral movement, persistence, and exfil. The AD tradecraft is the heart of it, Kerberos abuse, delegation, trusts, and AD CS.

## the exam

Hands-on in a live Windows domain. No multiple choice, no theory. You get lab access and a set of objectives, and you work the environment until you've pulled what the exam asks for. It's less about knowing the commands and more about keeping a foothold and moving laterally without tripping over your own tooling.

CRTL was the obvious next step.
